## What this project is

Fruit Fly Lingo is the interaction layer of the Embodied Drosophila Literacy Simulation (EDLS). It is a single static page (`index.html`, three.js loaded from jsdelivr, no build step) deployed on Vercel from this repository, `jayrosen-design/fly-lingo`, branch `main`.

- A procedural fruit fly with six IK legs, wings and a head-mounted compound-eye camera learns English morphology (prefix + root + suffix) on the screens people learn on.
- Eighteen experiments share one brain model and curriculum (18 Arcade: arcGroup cabinet with arcStick joystick and arcBtn A/B; arc holds the Root Blaster game drawn by drawArcade on arcCanvas (224 × 288, NearestFilter): bug formation with dives, centipede and mushrooms, capsules; stepArcade tilts the stick from arc.joy with both forelegs on the ball (tapLeg and tapLeg2, phase j_play); arcPlan picks the bug from game.memory; the caterpillar fires through arcCatStep, called from catStep; cabinet sides are ExtrudeGeometry slabs of PROFILE (z, y) with art faces painted by paintSideArt (mirror for the left side, lettering unmirrored); sfx(kind) synthesises the arcade effects on audio.ctx, rate-limited per kind, Experiment 18 only; Dashboard 18 d-arc. 17 Retro computer: retroGroup, a 1984 all-in-one styled after the original Macintosh with rtKeys keyboard and mouse; drawRetro paints the 1-bit 512 × 342 Morpho Typer game on rtCanvas (NearestFilter); stepRetro taps each letter with a foreleg (o_ phases) and rtFire scores the part with the shared game.memory; Dashboard 17 d-retro. 16 Mathematics: mathGroup with a TI-84-styled graphing calculator, tiKeys/tiCanvas/drawTI, and a children's calculator, kcKeys/drawKC; stepMath flies key to key, tail keys via tailOffset(TAIL_PITCH) and LEG_KEYS via legs[4]; genAlgebra, evalToks and calcResult compute what CALC shows from the typed tokens; the caterpillar types 4th grade problems through kidStep, called from catStep; Dashboard 16 d-math): 1 Walking (phone on a table), 2 Flying (climb to read, dive to answer, paths and landing heatmap), 3 Touch (fly-sized phone, swipe and tap), 4 Spelling (real-size laptop, fly key to key), 5 Teacher (fly-sized laptop, trackpad only, grades Flies A to D from a gradebook fed by the other experiments' real trials), 6 District admin (3 × 3 webcam grid of student simulations, vision overlay boxing flies, forelegs and gaze, FlyAI rule-based flags, seeded cheating scored for precision), 7 Classroom (24 student flies, 12 female and 12 male, at six round tables with a fly-sized tablet each in mating season: courtship with one-wing song, female rejection by ignoring, wing flick and kick or decamping, courtship conditioning lowering male drive, rival wing threats and lunges, grooming, glancing at neighbours, co-action; the fly stands on the mic facing the word, holds it down with a foreleg and sings each sound with one extended wing: pulse song for stops, formant-shaped hum for vowels, fluttered hiss for fricatives; - The experiment buttons, side-panel tabs and dashboard jump links show names only, no numbers.
- In the Simulate view the left dashboard panel, the right scenario panel and the bottom six-chart strip all switch per experiment (DOCK_FOR, data-rp sections and scenarioCard, STRIPS in index.html); teacher and admin show their laptop screen and work queue instead of the brain.
- A 57,000-point nervous system shaped after the FlyWire and MaleCNS neuropils (optic lobe layers, mushroom body lobes, central complex, glomerular antennal lobes, leg neuropils) lights up by region with per-neuron spikes as calcium flashes, an ellipsoid-body heading bump, optic flow, mushroom-body dopamine compartments, odour-specific glomeruli and per-leg hemineuromeres (`driveBrain` feeds the shader); the memory rule is Δw = η(R − V), η = 0.22; fifteen dashboards (kinematic, connectomic, psychometric with G-DINA, Half-Life Regression and a Rasch Wright map, curriculum with a toy mLSTM and Decision Transformer divergence, teacher navigation with Fitts' law, district proctoring, classroom social dynamics, phonics and wing song, reading stories aloud, writing Greek roots in cursive, VR classroom, AR word blocks, Scrabble, paper book, geography) run on live telemetry and also dock beside the arena.
- Caterpillar companion (butterfly mode, #catBtn, localStorage ffl-cat): CAT_EXPS 1, 9, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 (always on in 16 and 18); catSync shows it and catBuild paints the larva of bfMain() from CAT_LOOK; catStep crawls a chain of CAT_N segments in catZone, rearing to read; in the paper book catAttach/catRelease (from pbBeginTurn/pbEndTurn) carry it on the turning leaf via pbPts and mirror it onto the left page.
- Butterfly mode: the header's two logo buttons (data-species) call setSpecies; every wing group in WING_GROUPS carries the fly wing and a butterfly wing (addMorpho, wing sets from makeWingSet: MORPHO for the student and VR teacher, GNV_BUTTERFLIES, six Gainesville species, for the classroom); butterfly mode scales the fly by BF_SCALE 1.8 (classroom BF_SCALE_CLASS 1.3), and tailOffset scales with it, posed by morphoPose (closed at rest with basking, slow deep flaps, bobbing and weaving flight); fruit fly is the default and the choice is kept in localStorage.
- Layout: #metricsBtn toggles #stage.focus (setFocus), which hides the dock, strip and scenario panel so the arena fills the stage; on by default at 860px wide or less, kept in localStorage ffl-focus. The experiment .seg scrolls within .simctl so speed, camera and Play stay visible; setCamMode pulls the follow camera back on portrait screens.
- Controls: the Experiment dropdown (#expDrop, expMenuOpen) holds the data-exp buttons; camera 'back' (updateBackCam, povSubject) rides above the insect's thorax along the body's heading; #eyeBtn (setEye, localStorage ffl-eye) hides the eye inset; #bfSpecies (setBfSpecies, bfChoice, localStorage ffl-bf) rebuilds every butterfly wing with buildBf: 'mix' is Morpho plus the classroom's own species, a number is one wing set everywhere. Rendering: far 6000, fog 700 to 2600, fitClip sets the near plane from the viewing distance each frame, and textured PlaneGeometry decals get a polygon offset at start-up.
- Views: Home (default, experiment cards), Simulate, Dashboards, About (research library with the PDFs under `docs/research/`, all 187 cited sources, and the README's Mermaid architecture diagrams).
- Styling uses a University of Florida palette (core blue, dark blue, alachua, gator, bottlebrush) with Neulis Sans and Liebling and system fallbacks. Do not name the reading program the design tokens came from in the app or README.
- Planned back end per the EDLS requirement document: MuJoCo `flybody` via WebAssembly at 800 Hz, the Fly-connectomic Graph Model on MaleCNS v1.0, xLSTM with TFLA on WebGPU, xAPI telemetry through a Science DMZ to a Learning Record Store.

## Owner's interests and direction

Jay Rosen (University of Florida College of Education) is interested in exploring simulated brains, fruit fly connectomes first, to run realistic and sometimes humorous studies: teaching a fly to read, a fly grading its students, a fly proctoring other flies. The tone is serious about the measurement (real psychometrics, honest labelling of stand-ins) and playful about the premise. Prefer additions that make something new measurable over decoration. Every stand-in for a sensor or model the app does not yet have must be labelled as such in the UI.
